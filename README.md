# Superstore Sales Analysis (Excel)

Analysis of retail sales data (2015-2018) using Excel Pivot Tables.
Total sales: 2.26M.

## Questions and Findings
1. Region: West is the top region (31% of sales); South is the lowest (17%).
2. Category: Technology leads with 37%. Phones and Chairs are the top sub-categories.
3. Yearly trend: Sales grew about 51% from 2015 to 2018, with a small dip in 2016.
4. Monthly trend: Sales peak in Nov and Dec; Jan and Feb are weakest.
5. Segment: Consumer brings about 51% of sales.
6. Ship mode: Standard Class accounts for about 59% of sales.

## Data cleaning
- Removed 1 duplicate row.
